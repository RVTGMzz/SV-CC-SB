CARDCHA! CARD ICON ATLAS SPEC — v0.1.7

Approved art direction
----------------------
Fantasy-medallion pixel art:
- ornate gold framing
- strong readable silhouette
- jewel / badge language
- not overly feminine
- no card names or descriptions baked into images

Localization rule
-----------------
ALL names, rarity labels, descriptions, status text, and future translations stay runtime text.
The icon image contains visual art only.

Canonical atlas
---------------
File: assets/card_icons.png
Cell size: 64x64 px
Atlas size: 320x128 px
Grid: 5 columns x 2 rows
Background: transparent
Sampling: nearest-neighbor / pixel-art safe

Mapping
-------
0 Iron Edge
1 Thick Hide
2 Keen Eye
3 Swift Feet
4 Blood Fang
5 Executioner
6 Card Seeker
7 Chain Hunter
8 Last Stand
9 Phoenix Heart

Scaling strategy
----------------
The SAME 64x64 icon is reused for:
- Active Loadout
- Binder Collection
- Pull 1
- Pull 10
- future detail / tooltip layouts

No mini/full duplicate art files.

80-card scale
-------------
80 cards at 64x64 still fit comfortably in a small atlas family.
The renderer continues to draw frames, names, rarity, equipped badges,
duplicate status, buttons, and translations dynamically.
