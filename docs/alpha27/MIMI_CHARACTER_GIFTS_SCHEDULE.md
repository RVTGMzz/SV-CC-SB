# MiMi — Alpha.27 Character / Gift / Schedule

**Target branch:** `cardcha-alpha27-mimi-real-npc`

## Canon / live foundation

- Name: **MiMi**
- Birthday: **Spring 17**
- Friendship/social NPC unlocks after the Wizard-house meetup.
- Social tab + vanilla gifting are enabled after unlock.
- MiMi is not added to unrelated vanilla perfection/introduction requirements.
- Home location ID: `Cardcha_MiMiAttic`.
- Attic access unlock: **2 hearts**.
- Primary work window: **11:00–17:00, Monday–Friday**.
- Outside work hours and weekends: MiMi lives in the attic.

## Personality direction

MiMi is eccentric, independent, mysterious, slightly rushed, and follows her own odd logic. She values strange, magical, technical, or oddly specific objects more than raw market value. Aquarius ♒ is a personality inspiration; Spring 17 remains the Stardew birthday.

## One-button social interaction priority

When the player uses the normal Action/Confirm button near MiMi:

1. Holding a gift → Stardew handles gifting normally.
2. Not yet talked today → normal daily dialogue.
3. Already talked today + empty hand → open MiMi shop.

This is intentionally controller-friendly and avoids the shop stealing the gifting/talk button.

## Daily schedule

### After Wizard meetup, Community Center not restored

**Monday–Friday, clear weather**

- Before 11:00 → MiMi Attic
- 11:00–17:00 → Town merchant/wander routine
- 17:00 onward → MiMi Attic

**Monday–Friday, harsh weather** (rain/snow/lightning)

- Before 11:00 → MiMi Attic
- 11:00–17:00 → Wizard House
- 17:00 onward → MiMi Attic

**Saturday–Sunday**

- MiMi stays in MiMi Attic.

### After normal Community Center restoration

**Monday–Friday, clear weather**

- Before 11:00 → MiMi Attic
- 11:00–17:00 → Community Center
- 17:00 onward → MiMi Attic

Harsh weather currently redirects work to Wizard House.

### Joja route

Joja does not count as restored Community Center for MiMi's workplace, so her weekday public routine remains based around the Town merchant setup.

## Friendship / private-life progression direction

- 0–1 hearts → attic locked.
- 2 hearts → attic access.
- 4 hearts → more personal room dialogue/inspectables.
- 6 hearts → target unlock for the 17:30 private TV/romance secret routine.
- 8+ hearts → deeper MiMi / ChaCha / Card Dust / Community Center hooks.
